Add jobs from LinkedIn or Indeed

LinkedIn and Indeed don't allow automated scraping, so this app never pulls from them. You stay in control: bring in the jobs you find there.

1. Add a job you found

Open the posting on LinkedIn or Indeed, then copy its details here. Pasting the description gives a better match score and difficulty rating.

2. Bring in your LinkedIn saved or applied jobs

LinkedIn lets you download your own data: Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data, pick the jobs options, and download. Upload Saved Jobs.csv or Job Applications.csv below. The file is read once and not stored.

3. Search them yourself

Opens in a new tab, using your target titles and location from your profile.
